## The brief

Larkfield Private Wealth wants a home page with:

1. **The head:** lang, charset, viewport, a specific title, a meta description (120 to 160 characters) and an og:image.
2. **A header** with a `nav` (with an `aria-label`) holding at least three links that jump to sections on the page.
3. **A main** with one `h1` and a short intro paragraph.
4. **A hero image** in a `figure`, using `images/headland.svg`, with alt text, width, height and a caption.
5. **At least three sections**, each with an `id` and an `h2`. The nav links must point at them.
6. **A list**: the services (`ul`) or the steps (`ol`).
7. **The fee table** with a caption and column headers.
8. **An enquiry form** with labelled name and email fields and a submit button.
9. **A footer.**
