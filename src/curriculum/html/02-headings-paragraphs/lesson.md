Headings give a page its outline. Readers skim them, screen readers jump between them and Google uses them to understand what the page is about.

## Six levels

```html
<h1>Larkfield Private Wealth</h1>   <!-- the page title: only one -->
<h2>How we work</h2>                <!-- a main section -->
<h3>Your first meeting</h3>         <!-- a part of that section -->
```

There are six levels, `h1` to `h6`. Think of them like a book: `h1` is the book's title, `h2` is a chapter, `h3` is a section in that chapter.

**One rule that matters:** a page gets exactly **one** `h1`.
