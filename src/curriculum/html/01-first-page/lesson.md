## What HTML is

Every web page is a text file. HTML (HyperText Markup Language) wraps your words in **tags** that tell the browser what each piece *is*.

```html
<h1>This is the main heading</h1>
<p>This is a paragraph.</p>
```

A tag has an opening part `<h1>` and a closing part `</h1>` with a slash. Everything between them is the **content**. The opening tag, content and closing tag together make an **element**.

- `h1` means "heading level 1", the biggest, most important heading on the page.
- `p` means "paragraph".

## Your turn

1. In the editor, click on the empty line under the comment.
2. Type an `h1` with a heading about yourself, like `<h1>Sam is learning to code</h1>`.
3. Under it, type a `p` with one sentence about what you want to build.
4. Watch the preview on the right. It updates as you type.
5. Press **Check my work**.

> Tip: the lines between `<!--` and `-->` are **comments**. The browser ignores them. Use them to leave notes for yourself.
